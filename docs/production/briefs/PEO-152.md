# PEO-152 · Real-size stress cases from injected state: packed horde, saturated scent, twice play density, a late clock (D-059, D-060)

## Goal

The real-size tier covers the states a long game reaches that a 24-step walk from a fresh world never does: the whole horde packed round the player, scent at full strength on every open cell, more Dead than play density (so the threaded decision split runs), and a clock years after the Event. Each case injects its state directly instead of playing up to it, proves the update stays correct (speculate/commit equals step, executors agree, nothing wraps or saturates into nonsense) and reports its cost, since these are also the update's worst cases.

## Tests

- `a packed horde round the player commits exactly as step`: from realsize_params(), packed_layout puts all 11,650 Dead on the open cells nearest the player (about a 110-cell square); two Worlds load it; twin_walk 12 actions. After every update CHECK: horde size unchanged; occupied sums to the horde size and no cell holds more than one; every Dead on an open cell; every log_odds value in [-127, 127] or kNoChoice exactly where the move is walled; for every Dead whose cell has an open move, weights(pos) sums above zero (a crowd never leaves a unit with no draw)
- `scent at full strength everywhere stays bounded`: saturate_scent with params().scent.strength, then twin_walk 6 actions. After every update CHECK every sample lies in [0, strength] and every log_odds value in range; finally CHECK that a uniform field pulls nowhere: at every open cell farther than kWaveReachCells from the player with no Dead in its company boxes, every open move's log-odds is 0. Reports the full-field update time (the B1/B2 worst case)
- `twice play density: serial and threaded executors agree`: initial_dead = 2 x play_density_dead(1024, 1024) (23,300, over kParallelDecideMin), three Worlds: serial, ShuffledExecutor, and a real thread pool if the test harness has one; 12 actions; equivalent() after every action. CHECK the horde size equals initial_dead (no clamp ate units)
- `years after the Event the clock and attrition hold`: start_day = 365 x 30 (thirty years); twin_walk 12 actions; CHECK since_event() strictly increases by each action's substeps, every fate stays in range, and thin_horde of the horde at since_event() + one game year leaves no more Dead than now and never a negative or wrapped count
