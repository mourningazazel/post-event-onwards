# PEO-058 · The Dead move in staggered slots over a 9 s cycle (D-031)

## Goal

Per D-031. Every 9 s poll the movers and hash each a slot; at its slot a unit re-reads its target and decides, and the move lands one slot later; a vacated tile stays blocked until then; a calm unit facing another Dead stays put (no sidestep). Speed becomes slots per cycle (9 / step_seconds, integer guaranteed, fraction hashed), replacing PEO-042. Occupancy becomes a per-tile count grid; spawn avoids occupied tiles, so the pinned pre-clock hash is re-pinned on purpose. Speculation covers the Dead's slots up to the next update; commit re-decides units near logged tiles and follows any occupancy change forward in slot order. Measure per-update cost at 200x120 with 5000 Dead and in a one-wide corridor.
