# PEO-056 · Register the cat.* vocabulary; place or drop the twelve orphan archetypes

## Goal

Every cat.* tag has a registered family, every cat.* tag that a query names is carried by at least one item, and every concrete archetype is reachable from some room, loot table, outdoor set, profile or salvage yield, or is on a short allowlist of things the building generator places.

## Context

- Item-system review section 8 (vocabulary and orphans)
- content/registry/tags.toml [namespace.cat] (open today, no registered members; 363 distinct cat tags in use across about 40 families)
- docs/design/world-catalog.md section 10 (retail categories as tag queries)
- tools/content/lint.py check_tag, lint_world

## Units

- content: register each cat family as a tag (cat.clothing, cat.tool, cat.furniture, and so on: the second segment of every cat tag in use) with a one-line desc. namespace.cat stays open below the family, so cat.clothing.top needs no record of its own.
- tools: lint.py check_tag: a cat tag whose family is not registered is an error. A new check: every cat tag named in applies_to (tags_any, tags_all) of a modifier or detail table, in a loot or retail query, or in a room or building tags list, is carried by at least one item (prefix match: a query for cat.clothing is satisfied by cat.clothing.top). This is what catches a typo that would make an empty store.
- content: review the singleton families (cat.stock, cat.safety, cat.drink, cat.textile, cat.hauler) and merge any that are typos or near-duplicates of a larger family.
- tools: lint.py reachability: warn for each concrete item that no room object, contains, loose, loot entry, outdoor set, profile outfit, pockets or carried list, or salvage yield references. Allowlist in lint.py with a why: door_interior, door_exterior, door_steel, window_house, window_storefront (placed by the building generator).
- content: place or remove the twelve orphans measured on main: brick (construction_site outdoor set, garden loot), steel_wool (hardware_shelf_tools, garage_shelf), solar_charger (camping or electronics stock), elastic_bandage and burn_gel (pharmacy_otc, first-aid tables), ruler (desk_drawer, classroom_supplies), tin_snips (hardware_shelf_tools, workbench_tools), motorcycle_helmet (garage with a motorcycle, sporting_goods_stock), semi_trailer (highway_corridor, truck_stop), ammo_38spl (gun_store_stock, beside revolvers in nightstand and home safe), stun_gun_cartridge (beside stun_gun wherever it rolls), shed_object (backyard outdoor set, or remove if room.shed covers it). Removing is fine where an archetype has no home; say which in the commit.
