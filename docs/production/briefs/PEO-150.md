# PEO-150 · Save the continuous map: chunk records and their clocks; restore continues across recentres (PEO-118)

## Goal

A save holds the continuous map as played: the window's origin (WRLD, PEO-147), every chunk record with its clock and parked Dead, and the seen blocks (SEEN, PEO-148), so a restored world continues bit-identical through later recentres and returns; a save grows with the chunks visited, not with the map.

## Context

- Brief in full when PEO-149 lands: the record's final shape decides the layout. Outline below
- docs/adr/0017-save-evolution-fallbacks-and-sync-scopes.md (stored areas and area clocks are World scope); docs/architecture.md 'Planned seams' Save; world_save.cpp
- ADR-0007's generator versioning: the terrain generator's version belongs in WRLD with it

## Units

- core (outline): a CHNK section, World scope: records sorted by chunk, each its coord, left_at and units (chunk-local cell, speed, fate); restore checks every unit stands on an open cell of its chunk's regenerated terrain, no two on one cell, and no record lies inside the window
- core (outline): WRLD carries the terrain generator's version; a mismatch is refused with an issue
- test (outline): save after recentres, restore, walk back: equivalent to the world never saved; a CHNK unit on a wall is refused; save size grows with chunks visited
