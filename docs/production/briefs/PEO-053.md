# PEO-053 · Contents generator: compartments, per-compartment seeds, fill until full, freeze on search (D-023, D-026, D-027)

## Goal

Hidden contents become lines when a compartment is searched: one seed per compartment, independent picks, fill until the compartment's capacity is met, aftermath sampled from the address, lines frozen on search, nothing written for unsearched compartments. Acceptance is the item-system review's section 5.7 tests, with D-026's put-back rule replacing its stack round trip.
