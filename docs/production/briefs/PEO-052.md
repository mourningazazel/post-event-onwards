# PEO-052 · Content lint: loot versus container capacity; fix the eight mismatches; add storage_bin

## Goal

tools/content/lint.py fails when a loot table cannot fit the container it is rolled into (expected volume over capacity, or an entry whose packed longest dimension exceeds the container's max_dim_mm, or an object used as a container with no container feature), and every pairing in content passes after the fixes below.

## Context

- Item-system review, sections 4 and 8 (https://claude.ai/artifact/LHB7BW1sK7DpAof8o5dNkC); this brief carries everything needed from it
- docs/design/gameplay-model/G03-items-and-belongings.md D3.1 (volume + mass + longest dimension)
- docs/production/decisions.md D-023 (hidden per-container cap), D-026
- tools/content/lint.py lint_world (rooms, objects, contains); tools/content/derive.py (resolve_item, the executable spec)
- content/registry/shapes.toml (fabric, bag, cord are the flexible shapes)
- content/world/rooms/*.toml and content/world/loot/*.toml

## Units

- tools: derive.py gains packed_dims(db, item) -> [l, w, h]. Rigid shapes return dims_mm unchanged. fabric and bag fold: while the longest side exceeds kFoldTargetMm = 450, halve it and double the smallest side, re-sorting each time. cord coils: longest = max(kCoilMinMm = 150, ceil(sqrt(length_mm * girth_mm * 0.8))) where girth is the second dimension; the other two sides are that value and the girth. Name the constants at the top of derive.py with a one-line why each. Expectations in tests/content/expectations/derivations.toml: jeans packs to 525 or less, bedsheet to 325, nylon_rope (15240 x 12) to about 382, shotgun unchanged at 1200.
- tools: derive.py gains loot_expected(db, loot_id) -> (volume_ml, mass_g) as the mean over rolls, empty chance, entry weights and count ranges, recursing into nested loot, with volume from packed_dims. Round half away from zero with rnd(). Expectation: kitchen_drawer expected volume is within 10% of what the review measured (about 1.2 L).
- tools: lint.py, in lint_world, for every room object with contains: error if the object has no container feature; error if loot_expected volume exceeds capacity_ml; error if loot_expected mass exceeds max_mass_g when set; error for each loot entry whose packed longest side exceeds max_dim_mm when set. Worst-case overflow is not an error: generation fills until full (PEO-053), so capacity is the ceiling, not the table. Message names the room, the object, the loot table and the numbers.
- content: add item storage_bin (about 600 x 400 x 350 mm, 60 L polypropylene tote with a snap lid, cat.container.bin, from.garage, from.attic, from.bedroom) and use it instead of plastic_tub in room.attic, room.bedroom_child and room.storage_unit. plastic_tub stays the 2 L food tub.
- content: add item gun_safe (upright steel safe, about 1500 x 700 x 550 mm, container max_dim_mm about 1400, lock feature at a high tier) and use it for home_safe in the home rooms, for gun_store_stock in room.gun_counter and for police_armory_stock in room.police_armory. Keep safe for small valuables. Check the long-gun placement rate the home.toml header targets (about 1 in 3 homes) is unchanged.
- content: grocery_produce on metal_shelving and grocery_freezer_case on chest_freezer: reduce rolls or counts so the expected volume fits (a produce stand is one shelving section; a freezer case is one chest). warehouse_pallet: give pallet a container feature sized for one stacked pallet load (1219 x 1016 footprint, about 1500 mm stacked, about 1,800 L) and move warehouse_pallet from metal_shelving to pallet in room.warehouse_floor and room.stockroom. classroom_supplies on desk: split a teacher_desk table or give the classroom a supply cabinet; the student desk must not roll it.
- content: workbench gets a container feature (drawers and a lower shelf, about 150 L); dining_table used as a clothing display in room.clothing_floor becomes a display_table with a surface container, or the pairing moves to shelving.
- content: remaining longest-dimension failures after packing: move long rigid items (shovels, rakes, pitchforks, hockey sticks, bats, rifles on shelving, rebar, lumber, t-posts) out of the shelf and drawer tables into the room's loose list or a table the room places on the floor or a wall rack. Do not raise max_dim_mm past what the object physically allows.
- docs: content/README.md, the Checks section: one paragraph on the fit rule and on packed dimensions.
