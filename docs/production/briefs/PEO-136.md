# PEO-136 · Content: convert content/ to .peo and switch both loaders over (D-057)

## Goal

A one-time converter rewrites every TOML record as .peo under the approved root tree with full-path references, dims { l, w, h } and unit-carrying numbers; load_content and common.load read .peo (parents from header paths), toml++ and tomllib leave the content path, and a golden diff proves every resolved record is unchanged apart from the renames. Updates lint, derive, generate and test.py to the new field names.
