# PEO-130 · Content: generic parents for the remaining item families (D-046)

## Goal

Every remaining item family (clothing, furniture, fixtures, containers, vehicles, street furniture and the rest) has a plain spawnable generic parent added under PEO-116's add-nothing rule, checked with golden.py --unchanged-from. Families whose members share no composition part (trousers, jacket, boots, hat, chair, door, grill, van, key, glue, rope, pills, map, lumber, trash can, wrench) and families whose plain noun is already an item that would leak into its children (pliers, axe, safe, desk, flashlight, glass bottle) need a method first: rename child parts, leave them parentless (removal becomes junk), or change how resolution merges; raise a D-xxx if it is a direction call.
