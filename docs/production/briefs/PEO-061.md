# PEO-061 · soak_rate: bedding without a seat counts as sleep contact (D-013, D-028)

## Goal

Bedding a body lies on for hours soaks like a bed. Today derive.py counts sleep contact only through seat.sleepable, so sleeping_bag and bedsheet derive 7 (a t-shirt's value) against a mattress's 40.

## Context

- PEO-055 report (commit da5983a), open item 3.
- D-013 soak targets: ten days of house life ~75-80%, camping one spot ~30%; D-028 portable absorbers carry soak on the thing.

## Units

- tools: derive.py adds the sleep term (kSoakSleep) for items tagged as bedding (sleeping_bag, bedsheet, blanket, pillow and any cat tag family that means bedding), with a one-line why; keep the textile doubling.
- content: pin the new values in tests/content/expectations/derivations.toml.

## Acceptance

- sleeping_bag and bedsheet derive between 20 and 40 and stay portable; mattress stays 40; t_shirt stays 7
- lint 0 errors; test.py passes

## Tests

- expectations for sleeping_bag, bedsheet, blanket, pillow

## Out of scope

- engine soak (PEO-038)
- changing kSoak constants other than adding the bedding rule
