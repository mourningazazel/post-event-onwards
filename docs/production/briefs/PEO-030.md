# PEO-030 · Geodesic scent field: integer, propagating, min-plus commit patch (D-024)

## Goal

Per D-024. The world's scent is an integer geodesic field: each cell holds strength - C x route distance - K x age, propagated `speed` cells per update along walkable, non-corner-cutting routes, bounded by strength. On the town fixture (PEO-035) a standing player's scent reaches every cell within reach and the Dead climb to the player from all of them; a Dead following a walked trail arrives instead of parking at a crossing; speculate and commit stay bit-identical.

## Units

- core: src/core/include/peo/core/scent_wave.hpp and src/core/src/scent_wave.cpp. `struct WaveParams { std::int32_t strength; std::int32_t distance_cost; std::int32_t age_cost; int speed = 1; }` with named defaults kWaveDistanceCost = 8, kWaveAgeCost = 8, kWaveReachCells = 60 and strength = kWaveReachCells * (distance_cost + age_cost). `class ScentWave`: int32 value grid, a changed-cells list and its next-round twin, a uint32 stamp grid, an update counter. deposit(Vec2i, std::int32_t strength) sets value = age_cost * updates + strength if higher and marks the cell changed. update(const Grid<bool>& blocked) increments the counter and runs `speed` rounds: each changed cell offers value - distance_cost to its 8 neighbours that are open and not reached by cutting a corner (both orthogonal cells open, as PEO-044 rules for the Dead), and a neighbour that improves, and whose offer is still above the aged zero line, joins the next round. sample(Vec2i) returns max(0, value - age_cost * updates). strongest_neighbour(from, blocked) has ScentField's contract on sampled values. No allocation after warm-up: lists are reserved and reused.
- test: tests/core/test_scent_wave.cpp: a deposit on an open grid gives sample = strength - distance_cost * chebyshev distance after enough updates; a wall line absorbs (nothing behind it without a route round); a one-wide corridor carries the value down its length losing exactly distance_cost per cell; the field ages by age_cost per update with no deposit; nothing is ever read past strength / (distance_cost + age_cost / speed) cells; speed 2 advances the front two cells per update; no corner cutting.
- core: World switches from ScentField to ScentWave. WorldParams replaces `ScentParams scent` and `float player_scent` with `WaveParams scent`. deposit_log: a tile occupied s seconds of the period deposits strength - age_cost * (period - s) / period (integer), so a walking step deposits exactly strength and a runner's two 3 s tiles each deposit a little less, never more (D-015). run_update: deposit the logged tiles, then update(). speculate: copy the wave and run update() with no deposit. finish_from: deposit the logged tiles into the speculated wave and run `speed` rounds from those cells alone (min-plus superposition, the review's section 4 argument); keep the re-decide box and radius rules as they are. Dead: step_dead and step_horde take `const ScentWave&`; nothing else in the Dead changes. World::equivalent compares the int grid and counters. Keep ScentField, scent.hpp and test_scent.cpp in the tree, unused by World, until this item is accepted; their removal becomes a follow-up chore.
- test: 'the pre-clock world is pinned' is re-pinned once, deliberately, and its comment says why (D-024 changed the field). 'commit(speculate()) is bit-identical to step()' and its mixed 3/6/12 s variant pass unchanged in form. Update test_dead.cpp to build its gradients with ScentWave.
- test: in test_town.cpp, 'the geodesic field on the town': a standing deposit in the deepest office room for kWaveReachCells + 1 updates; every open cell with bfs distance <= reach has sample > 0; climb_all from those cells succeeds from 100% with stretch 1.0 and 0 frozen. 'a Dead follows a trail into the house': the player walks walk_path from the top street to the deepest room one tile per update, then waits 50 updates; a Dead starting on the path 5 updates behind ends within 1 cell of the player. 'the doorway scenario' (D-008's old acceptance): linger 30 updates inside the front door of a house, walk 20 cells into the house, and a Dead that starts outside reaches the door.
- app: src/app/main.cpp's scent view reads sample() as an int and bands it by fraction of the current maximum, as today; only the type changes. No logic in the app.
- docs: docs/architecture.md, the scent paragraph: the geodesic field, its three numbers, the min-plus patch, and that reach bounds the work (D-024). docs/design/scent-performance.md gains a short section with the new timings; mark the IIR section superseded by D-024.

## Acceptance

- on the town fixture: reach and climb both 100% within the reach radius; the trail and doorway scenarios pass
- commit(speculate()) is bit-identical to step(), including the mixed-duration variant
- a runner never deposits more on a tile than a walker; walking deposits exactly strength
- speculate and commit at 200x120 with 5000 Dead are no slower than main (report both from headless-release, before and after)
- no allocation per update after warm-up; the suite stays under 1 s
