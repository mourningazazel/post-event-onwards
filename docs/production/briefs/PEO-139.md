# PEO-139 · Items read through the seed: take one instantiates, put back lists beside, examined mark, quality sampled (D-023, D-026, D-029)

## Goal

Split from PEO-050. An implied unit in a line reads its properties through its archetype and its address; taking one, examining it or changing it instantiates it as a Thing with its address (line address, unit index), quality sampled from the line's mean and spread by a counter-based hash (D-029); put back it lists as its own entry beside the nameless quantity, never merged (D-026); an entry not yet examined carries a mark the frontend can show.
