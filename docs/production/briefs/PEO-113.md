# PEO-113 · Shore and river buildings, rooms and items in the census (ADR-0018 point 2)

## Goal

Docks, boat sheds, bait shops, lakeside houses, breakwaters, flood defences and river-trade buildings exist in content/world as buildings, rooms and items with shore and river tags and census weights, data only (D-042: the owner writes their descriptions), so a layout can ask for shore or river content by tag.
