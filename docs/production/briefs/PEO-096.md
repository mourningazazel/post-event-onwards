# PEO-096 · Towns fitted to geography (ADR-0018)

## Goal

peo::core places settlement sites on a generated geography region (ADR-0018 point 3, first half): one candidate site per site cell (an eighth of a square a side, 12.5 km), chosen from hashed candidate points by an integer score that prefers flat, low land and landmarks (sea coast, lake shore, mountains in view), each site carrying a size class, its radius, its landmark and which water (sea, lake) lies within its limits. A site reads only the region it sits in and stays inside its site cell, so the same seed gives the same sites in any generation order. peo_geo draws them. This is the first of PEO-096's split: river relation and the river-town share are PEO-112, shore and river content PEO-113, the town layout generator PEO-114, layouts fitted to water PEO-115. Nothing in game changes: stages do not read sites.

## Units

- core: peo/core/settlement_site.hpp + src/core/src/settlement_site.cpp, the types. enum class SiteSize : std::uint8_t { Hamlet, Village, Town, City }; enum class Landmark : std::uint8_t { None, Sea, Lake, Mountains } (PEO-112 adds River); water bits inline constexpr std::uint8_t kSiteSea = 1, kSiteLake = 2 (4 and 8 documented as reserved for PEO-112's river beside and through). struct SiteParams, every tunable named with its default and unit: site_cells_per_side = 8 (site cell side = kSquareCells / 8 = 125 cells, 12.5 km; site cells tile the world from cell 0 by floor division, so each lies inside one square); site_chance_q16 (share of site cells holding a site; owner: long empty stretches, towns about 20 km apart); candidates_per_cell = 16; margin_cells = 40 (candidate centres jitter only within [margin, side - margin) of their cell); per SiteSize a radius_cells (start 3, 6, 15, 35: 0.3 to 3.5 km) and a weight (start 40, 35, 20, 5); max_site_elevation_m; min_land_q16 (share of the disc that must be land); relief weight (points per metre between the lowest and highest land cell in the disc); altitude weight (points per metre above sea level at the centre); sea_bonus, lake_bonus, mountain_bonus and mountain_rise_m (the highest land cell in the site cell at least this far above the centre); jitter_points (a hashed tie-breaker added to each score); min_score. constexpr bool valid(const SiteParams&): side divides kSquareCells, margin at least the largest radius, 2 x margin below the side, at least one candidate, weights not all zero, Q16 shares inside [0, 1 << 16]. struct SettlementSite { std::uint64_t key; std::int64_t site_x, site_y (site cell); std::int64_t cell_x, cell_y (centre, world cells); SiteSize size; std::int32_t radius_cells; std::int16_t elevation_m; Landmark landmark; std::uint8_t water; std::uint64_t lake_key (0 when none: the first lake body by bodies order with a cell in the disc); std::int32_t score; }. Write each worst case beside its type (int64 for sums over a disc)
- core: the functions, pure and allocation only in the result. [[nodiscard]] CellRect site_cell_rect(const SiteParams&, std::int64_t site_x, std::int64_t site_y) noexcept. [[nodiscard]] std::vector<CellCorner> site_candidates(Seed world_seed, const SiteParams&, std::int64_t site_x, std::int64_t site_y): the candidates_per_cell centres, each jitter from hash_u64 of a named salt and the site cell and candidate index. [[nodiscard]] std::optional<std::int32_t> score_site(const SiteParams&, const GeographyRegion&, std::int64_t cell_x, std::int64_t cell_y, std::int32_t radius_cells) noexcept: nullopt when the centre is sea or lake, above max_site_elevation_m, or the disc's land share is below min_land_q16; else the bonuses for sea and lake cells inside the disc and for mountains in the site cell, minus the relief and altitude terms. [[nodiscard]] std::vector<SettlementSite> place_sites(Seed world_seed, const SiteParams&, const GeographyRegion&): for every site cell whose whole rect lies inside region.rect, in (site_y, site_x) order: the site cell's chance roll, then its size from a hashed weighted draw, then each candidate scored at that size's radius plus its hashed jitter; the best (ties to the lower candidate index) is the site if its score is at least min_score. Landmark is the largest bonus the winner took (ties Sea, Lake, Mountains), None if it took none. key = hash_u64(world_seed, kSaltSite, packed site cell). Reads only region cells inside the site cell, so the result is the same whatever region holds the cell (a test pins it). Any helper over the disc may visit every cell; note the cost per square in the report
- test: tests/core/test_settlement_site.cpp with TEST_SUITE("settlement sites") for the unit cases and TEST_SUITE("scenario: settlement sites") for the generated-square cases (cases under Tests), added to tests/CMakeLists.txt. Unit cases use hand-built GeographyRegions (one or four site cells of flat land, a lake block with its WaterBody and body ids, a sea edge, a slope) so they pin the rules without the terrain noise; the golden hash case is written last, after the peo_geo review
- test: peo_geo gains --sites: runs place_sites with default SiteParams over each generated square and draws each site as a ring at its radius, coloured by size, with a centre dot; prints per square the site count by size and by landmark, the share of sites with sea, with lake and with no water, and the place_sites time in ms
- docs: docs/architecture.md 'Geography (ADR-0018, PEO-095)' gains a short 'Settlement sites' paragraph (site cells inside squares, candidates and the score, reads only its cell, what is not built: rivers PEO-112, layouts PEO-114/115) and adds settlement_site.hpp to the 'Current code' map; docs/design/world-generation.md 'Owner answers' row 'Settlement siting' gains one line pointing at peo/core/settlement_site.hpp

## Manual

- Nothing in game changes: build dev, run ./build/dev/bin/peo, walk through a stage exit, confirm play looks as before
- Run peo_geo --seed 1 --square -1,-1 --span 3 --scale 3 --grid --sites --out sites.ppm (and seeds 2 and 3). Look for: towns spread with long empty stretches between them, none on water or high in the mountains, coasts and lake shores taking more than their share, valleys preferred over slopes, larger towns rarer. Tune SiteParams defaults until it reads as settled land, then pin the scenario bands and the golden. Say in the note what you tuned and why
- Not a per-update path (no docs/bottlenecks.md row): sites are placed once per square, far ahead of the player. Report place_sites ms per square in headless-release from peo_geo's output
