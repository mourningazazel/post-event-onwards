# PEO-009 · Weighted-draw movement for the Dead with aggregates and company pull

## Goal

A calm Dead's step becomes a weighted random draw over its open neighbours and staying (scent, stay and company terms), surer on a trail as the scent gets stronger (D-038 B), so a crowd fans out where scent is flat, accumulates where it is strong and climbs a fresh trail almost greedily; deterministic, integer, and commit(speculate()) still bit-identical to step()

## Context

- docs/production/decisions.md D-038 (B): the lean toward climbing scent grows with the scent's strength; two knobs (at the edge of reach, at full strength) with an integer gradient between. Ryan: keep to the gradient mindset, no bands or fixed cases
- docs/design/scent-mobs.md round 3 'One movement rule for every one of the Dead' and 'Reproducible swarm tests': the term table and the swarm-accumulation and fan-out scenarios. Idle Dead with no scent keep a random 8-direction step (round 1); stay has its own weight
- D-031 binds (decisions.md, src/core/src/world.cpp poll/dead_second): the draw ignores occupancy; if the drawn tile is taken or reserved the unit stays and does not draw again. Calm Dead never share a tile, never sidestep, never push. Slots stay as plan_slots gives them, but every unit now gets its slots (wanderers too), not only units with a stronger neighbour
- Field values are linear in route (D-024: strength - distance_cost x route - age_cost x age), so a fixed per-step preference would be as sure at the edge as beside the source; that is why the lean scales with sample(from). The weight for neighbour n can be read as 2^(lean(sample(from)) x (sample(n) - sample(from)) / distance_cost), lean rising from the edge knob to the full knob; method is yours, integers only (D-021)
- D-037 and docs/research/parallel-and-gpu.md section 6 item 6: terms that read only the last update (scent, company now; aggregate, repellent, footing later) are baked into a per-cell desire field in a dense pass after each update, so a decision is a lookup plus a draw. Unlike the PEO-079 direction byte, the lean depends on the absolute strength, which the age line lowers every update, so weights change over the whole reached area each update, not only where values changed
- Randomness: hash of (salt, cycle, unit index, slot) as plan_slots does (src/core/src/dead.cpp), with its own salt so it never reuses plan_slots' words; never a shared stream (PEO-007 note)
- PEO-080 runs first: its intents pass (each unit writes a target, claims resolve per tile to the lowest index) is where the draw goes, and the desire field's dense pass takes the executor

## Units

- core: a DeadDrawParams struct in WorldParams with named knobs: climb lean at the edge of reach and at full strength (as odds per distance_cost step of climb), stay weight (against a level neighbour), company gain and radius. Defaults chosen so the scenarios below pass; record them with the measured shares in their comments
- core: the desire field: per open cell, integer weights for the eight neighbours (blocked cells and cut corners weigh 0, PEO-044) and stay, from the scent term and the company term (a box sum of the occupancy snapshot taken at the update, in the direction of each neighbour). Built in a dense pass after each update over the cells whose weights can differ from the uniform wander weights; patched at commit over the block patch_deposit changed plus its ring. No allocation once warm
- core: decide_move becomes the draw: total the unit's cell weights, pick by hash, return the tile or stay; D-031's taken-or-reserved rule unchanged. poll gives every unit its slots
- test: unit tests for the weights (monotone in climb, lean rising with strength, zero into walls and cut corners, uniform where scent and company are flat) and for the draw's determinism (same seed, same draws; independent of horde order)
- test (scenario tier): sure-footed gradient, swarm-accumulation and fan-out as below; existing Dead and World tests that assert a greedy step are rewritten to the draw's behaviour and the goldens repinned, each change citing D-038 in the commit
- docs: scent-mobs.md round 3 notes what is built (terms, knobs, desire field) and what waits

## Acceptance

- verify passes; unit tier under 1 s and scenario tier under 5 s (D-033)
- Sure-footed gradient (open stage, one standing emitter, 2,000 Dead, a fixed number of cycles): the share of moves that climb is at least 90% within 10 cells of the emitter, below 60% beyond 45 cells, and rises with each 5-cell band in between (a gradient, not a step). Report the shares per band
- swarm-accumulation: an even spread of Dead with one region of stronger scent ends with a share in that region at least 1.5x its starting share. fan-out: a tight cluster on flat scent covers at least 3x its starting bounding area with no tile ever holding two Dead. Report both metrics
- commit(speculate()) is bit-identical to step() in every existing equivalence test, calm and windy; no Dead ever enters a wall or shares a tile
- headless-release, serial and 4 workers: 512x512 with 20,000 and 50,000 Dead, speculate and commit before and after. Speculated update under 100 ms and commit under 1 ms on the M1 (D-021); report the desire field's memory per cell and at 1024x1024

## Manual

- Play on the default stage: Dead near your trail close in steadily; Dead far out drift and shuffle with a lean your way; a packed group spreads rather than freezing in a line
- Stand still for a minute: the nearest Dead arrive about as fast as before; the far ones arrive later and more spread out
