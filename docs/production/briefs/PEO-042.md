# PEO-042 · Dead speed: probabilistic extra steps per update (D-022)

## Goal

Per D-022. A Dead's expected steps per update is update_period / step_seconds as a real number: for step_seconds below the period the integer part is guaranteed and the fraction is the chance of one more step, drawn from a counter-based hash, capped at kMaxDeadStepsPerUpdate = 6. Slow Dead (step_seconds at or above the period) keep today's cooldown behaviour exactly, so every existing seed plays bit for bit as before.

## Units

- core: rng.hpp gains a free function `std::uint64_t hash_u64(std::uint64_t a, std::uint64_t b, std::uint64_t c) noexcept` (splitmix64 finaliser over a, b and c folded with the golden-ratio constant, same constants as reseed) and `float hash_unit(...)` mapping it to [0, 1) as unit() does. Doctest in tests/core/test_rng.cpp: same inputs give the same value; changing any one argument changes it; the mean of 10,000 hash_unit draws over consecutive counters is within 0.02 of 0.5.
- core: dead.hpp names `constexpr int kMaxDeadStepsPerUpdate = 6`. step_dead gains `std::uint64_t salt` and `std::size_t index` (the Dead's position in the horde) after `period`. If step_seconds >= period, behave exactly as today (cooldown_s path). Else: guaranteed = period / step_seconds; extra = hash_unit(salt, index, 0) < float(period % step_seconds) / step_seconds ? 1 : 0; take min(guaranteed + extra, kMaxDeadStepsPerUpdate) greedy steps in sequence, each from the new position, reading the same scent field; stop early when no neighbour is stronger. cooldown_s stays 0 on this path. Returns whether it moved at all; step_horde passes salt and the loop index.
- core: World computes salt once per update as hash_u64(seed_, updates_, stage_index_) and passes it to step_horde in run_update and to step_dead in finish_from with the same index i, so commit(speculate()) stays bit-identical. Widen the re-decide box and near(): a unit within kRepatchRadius + (kMaxDeadStepsPerUpdate - 1) cells of a logged tile is re-decided, since a fast unit's later steps may read patched cells.
- core: WorldParams gains `Seconds min_step_seconds = kUpdatePeriodSeconds` and `Seconds max_step_seconds = 3 * kUpdatePeriodSeconds`, replacing kMinDeadSpeed/kMaxDeadSpeed; spawn draws rng_.range over that inclusive range in seconds. Defaults reproduce today's draw exactly (the pinned hash must not change). World::equivalent is unchanged.
- test: tests/core/test_dead.cpp: a unit with step_seconds 3 on a 6 s period takes exactly 2 steps up a straight gradient every update; with step_seconds 4 it takes 1 or 2, and over 1,000 updates with rising salts the mean is within 0.05 of 1.5; with step_seconds 1 it takes 6, never 7; the same salt and index always give the same count.
- test: tests/core/test_world.cpp: a golden variant of 'commit(speculate()) is bit-identical to step()' with min_step_seconds 2 and max_step_seconds 12 on the mixed 3/6/12 s script, and 'the pre-clock world is pinned' still passes with its pinned value.
- docs: docs/architecture.md, the paragraph on the Dead's update: expected steps per update, the guaranteed part and the chance part, the cap, and that the draw is a hash of (seed, update, index), never a shared stream (D-022). Two sentences.

## Acceptance

- the pinned pre-clock hash passes unchanged: default spawns are slow Dead and play exactly as before
- commit(speculate()) is bit-identical to step() with fast Dead present (the golden variant)
- a unit with step_seconds 3 takes 2 steps per 6 s update; step_seconds 4 averages 1.5; step_seconds 1 takes 6, never more
- no allocation per update; step_horde at 200x120 with 5000 Dead stays within 10% of main when all Dead are slow (report both numbers from headless-release)
- the suite stays under 1 s
