# PEO-035 · Turn cost and Dead movement in cramped layouts: houses, offices, corridors

## Goal

Know the worst-case cost of one turn, and whether the Dead still find the player, on congested layouts (narrow halls, many small rooms, doors) at realistic sizes. Per the user (2026-09-29): exhaust math-centric approaches first (e.g. Dijkstra/flow fields, fast marching, precomputed per-stage structure, sparse or incremental updates, the PEO-030 IIR layer) before proposing any change to gameplay mechanics; a mechanics change is the user's call. If a turn is too slow, look at spreading or precomputing the next turn's work (see PEO-007).
