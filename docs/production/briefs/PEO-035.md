# PEO-035 · Town fixture: a cramped 200x120 test map with reach, climb and trail helpers

## Goal

A deterministic town map and three measuring helpers live in the test tree, so PEO-030 and every later scent change are judged on houses, offices and a one-wide corridor, not only on the open stage.

## Context

- docs/production/decisions.md (D-024), docs/design/notes/N017
- pathfinding review, /mnt/project-files/reviews/2026-09-29-pathfinding-and-scent-review.md, sections 3 and 4 (what the town map showed and why)
- /mnt/project-files/reviews/probes/probe.cpp: make_town (line 28), bfs_dist (74), climb_all (95); port these, do not link the probe
- tests/CMakeLists.txt, tests/core/test_world.cpp (kSeed, how cases are laid out)

## Units

- test: tests/core/town_fixture.hpp, header-only, namespace peo::test. `Grid<bool> make_town(Rng& rng, bool cramped)` ported from the probe: 200x120, streets, four-room houses, office blocks with a one-cell-wide corridor; blocked = true for walls. Named constants for every size; no magic numbers.
- test: in the same header, `std::vector<int> bfs_dist(const Grid<bool>&, Vec2i source)` (8-connected, no corner cutting, -1 for unreachable) and `ClimbResult climb_all(const Grid<bool>&, const std::vector<int>& dist, Vec2i goal, Sample sample, int limit)` returning the share of reachable cells from which a strict strongest-neighbour walk arrives at `goal`, the mean path stretch against geodesic distance, and the count of walks that froze. Sample is any callable Vec2i -> comparable value, so it works for the float field today and the integer field in PEO-030.
- test: in the same header, `std::vector<Vec2i> walk_path(const Grid<bool>&, Vec2i from, Vec2i to)`: a shortest route by bfs_dist, used for the trail scenario.
- test: tests/core/test_town.cpp (add to tests/CMakeLists.txt): 'the town fixture is deterministic' (same seed, same grid bits), 'every open cell is reachable from the top street', 'the cramped town has a one-wide corridor at least 8 cells long ending in a room', and 'climb_all on an exact geodesic sample climbs from every cell' (sample = -dist; success 1.0, stretch 1.0, frozen 0), which proves the helper before any field depends on it.
- test: in test_town.cpp, 'baseline: the diffusion field on the town' under #ifdef NDEBUG: a standing deposit in the deepest office room for 300 updates, MESSAGE the reached share and climb share. No assertion on those two numbers; this is the before-picture PEO-030 improves on. Assert only that it ran.
