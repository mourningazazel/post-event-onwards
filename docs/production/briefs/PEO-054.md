# PEO-054 · Compartments feature and cabinet runs; home room programs fit their tiles

## Goal

Furniture and fixtures declare compartments (each drawer, shelf or cupboard with its own capacity, longest dimension, lock and loot hook), each compartment has a derived search time from its volume (D-027), kitchens use cabinet runs, and content lint proves no room program places more furniture than its floor holds.

## Context

- docs/design/notes/N017 (the owner's words on tedium and fast menus)
- Item-system review section 5.5 (compartments as a feature) and section 4 (kitchens place 12 to 25 objects in 8 to 25 m2)

## Units

- content: registry property compartments, type list<table>, readers [generation, M10 access, search]: each entry { name (str, shown in the search menu), capacity_ml, max_mass_g?, max_dim_mm?, closable?, lock? (same params as the lock feature), loot? (default loot id) }. Lint: an item has either features.container or compartments, never both; names are unique within the item; lock params validate against feature.lock.
- tools: derive.py search_seconds(capacity_ml) = kSearchQuantumS * ceil(sqrt(capacity_ml / kSearchBaseMl)), with kSearchQuantumS = 6 (one walking step, D-014) and kSearchBaseMl = 12000 (a kitchen drawer takes one quantum). A single-container item uses its container capacity. Expectations: a 12 L drawer 6 s, a 120 L cupboard 24 s, a 700 L shelving unit 48 s, a 60 L hiking pack 18 s.
- content: compartments on dresser (six drawers), nightstand (a drawer and a cupboard), desk (a pedestal of drawers with a lock and a centre drawer), filing_cabinet (four drawers, one lock for all: the top one carries it), bookshelf (five shelves), wardrobe (hanging rail and a shelf), metal_shelving (five shelves), kitchen_cabinet (a drawer and a cupboard). Capacities sum to about today's container capacity; loot hooks carry today's contains tables.
- content: new fixtures kitchen_base_run (a 1 m base cabinet tile: two drawers and a cupboard), kitchen_wall_run (a 1 m wall cupboard above a counter tile, two shelves; placed on the same tile as a base run, blocks = none), kitchen_pantry (a tall cupboard, four shelves) and kitchen_sink_base (one cupboard, loot under_sink). Room kitchen places runs by count of wall tiles instead of eleven kitchen_cabinet objects: base runs 3 to 8, wall runs over most of them, pantry 0 to 1, sink base 1.
- content: room objects may override a compartment's loot with loot = { <compartment name> = <loot id> }; contains stays valid only for single-container items and lint errors on contains for an item with compartments. Room kitchen overrides the pantry shelves to pantry_shelf and the base-run drawers to kitchen_drawer.
- tools: lint.py room fit. Each object with blocks partial or full, or fixed, takes ceil(length / 1000) x ceil(width / 1000) tiles; wall runs and wall-mounted fixtures (blocks none) take none. Error if the sum of minimum counts of objects with no chance_pct exceeds kRoomFillMax = 0.6 of the minimum size_m2, or the sum of maximum counts exceeds 0.6 of the maximum size_m2. The generator (PEO-053) draws counts proportional to the rolled area inside those ranges, so both ends must fit.
- content: fix every room the fit rule fails. Measured on main: home bathroom, bedroom_child, bedroom_master, garage, home_office, kitchen, laundry_room, shed; also bar_room, break_room, conference_room, control_room, dining_area, emergency_bay, holding_cell, hotel_room, motel_room, nurse_office, pharmacy_counter, police_armory, police_bullpen, storage_unit, walk_in_cooler. Prefer narrowing count ranges or raising a room's size range to realistic values over dropping objects; a bedroom of 9 m2 holds a bed, a dresser and a nightstand.
- tools: PEO-052's fit check runs per compartment against that compartment's loot, and the room-level contains check moves to it.
- docs: content/README.md item record: the compartments example (the kitchen_base_run above) and the loot override; docs/design/gameplay-model/G03 D3.1: one sentence that furniture holds compartments and each is searched on its own (D-027).
