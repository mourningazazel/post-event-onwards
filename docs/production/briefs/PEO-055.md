# PEO-055 · Derived soak_rate per archetype in derive.py (D-013, D-028)

## Goal

tools/content/derive.py derives a soak_rate multiplier per archetype as a gradient from floor (1x) through hard furniture to bedding (about 40x), from material, surface and contact features, and marks portable absorbers (D-028), pinned by content expectations, so PEO-038 reads one number per object.

## Context

- docs/production/decisions.md D-013 (soak, furniture about 37x floor), D-014 (per-second rates), D-028 (soak travels with portable absorbers)
- Item-system review section 7 (the rate table and the moving-object rule)
- tools/content/derive.py main_material, capabilities; content/registry/properties.toml absorbs_scent, fixed; content/registry/features.toml seat, surface
- content/materials/*.toml (family, surface)

## Units

- tools: derive.py soak_rate(db, item) -> int, a multiplier of the floor rate, integer so the engine port is exact. Start from contact: kSoakBase = 1; + kSoakSurface (5) if features.surface; + kSoakSeat (12) if features.seat; + kSoakSleep (20) if seat.sleepable. Scale by absorbency of the main material family and surface: porous or fibrous surfaces and textile or foam families x2, smooth metal, glass, ceramic or stone x0.5 (floor never below 1). Add + kSoakAbsorber (4) when absorbs_scent. Clamp to [1, kSoakMax = 45]. Names and a one-line why each at the top of derive.py. Adjust constants so the expectations below hold; they are the spec, the formula is a starting point.
- tools: derive.py soak_portable(db, item) -> bool: true when the item is not fixed, weighs at most WIELD_MAX_G x 5 (a mattress can be dragged; a sofa cannot be carried but its cushions can), and absorbs_scent or its main family is textile, or it has a sleepable seat. D-028: a portable absorber carries its soak as a value on the thing.
- content: set absorbs_scent = true on mattress, sofa, armchair and bedding items where it is missing, so the derivation sees them; keep false on hard furniture.
- test: expectations in tests/content/expectations/derivations.toml: mattress 37 to 45; sofa 25 to 30; dining_chair 12 to 18; dining_table 6 to 10; metal_shelving 1 to 2; t_shirt at least 4 and portable; mattress portable; sofa not portable; kitchen_cabinet not portable.
- docs: add soak_rate and soak_portable to the derived list in docs/design/content-model.md section 2.4, one line each, citing D-013 and D-028; note on PEO-038 that the per-object multiplier is soak_rate x the floor charge rate.
