# PEO-112 · Settlement sites know their rivers; river-town share as data (ADR-0018 point 4)

## Goal

Each settlement site from PEO-096 records whether a river runs through or beside it and its size class, scores river confluences as a landmark, and a hashed per-site roll against river_town_share (a data value, ADR-0018 proposed start about 1 in 5) decides whether a site may take a river, so most towns have none within their limits; the scenario test pins the realised share.
