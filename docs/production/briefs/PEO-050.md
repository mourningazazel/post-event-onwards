# PEO-050 · Items as seeds: read through the seed, instantiate on change, resolve on access, hidden container cap (D-023)

## Goal

Per D-023. An unopened container is a seed; an item reads its properties through its seed until a property changes, then becomes a unique record; time-driven change resolves from the clock on access, name first; a hidden, very high per-container count cap refuses adds but never blocks growth from a stack. The item-system review's contents generator (compartments, per-compartment seeds, fill until full) builds on this; D-026 to D-029 refine it. Stub by intent; brief after PEO-008 and the item review's answers.
