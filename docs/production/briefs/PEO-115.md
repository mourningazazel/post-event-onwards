# PEO-115 · Town layouts fitted to rivers and shores: bridges, riverside streets, shore-bent grid (ADR-0018 point 3)

## Goal

A town on a river or shore is laid out around it: bridges where streets cross a river, riverside streets, a street grid bent along a lake or sea shore, and shore and river buildings, rooms and items (PEO-113) placed on the lots that face the water.
