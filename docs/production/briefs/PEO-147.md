# PEO-147 · World is a window over the one map: no exit, no stage advance, no stage index (PEO-118)

## Goal

A generated World is a chunk-aligned window over the one map: its terrain read from map_terrain at the window's world origin, with no border walls, no exit and no stage advance; the player starts on the open cell nearest world cell (0, 0); stage indices are gone from core, the save and the app.

## Units

- core: generated windows. WorldParams' stage_width / stage_height are the window's sides: a generated world rounds each up to whole chunks and to at least kMinWindowChunks (3, so 96 cells), named in world.hpp; load_layout keeps any size from kMinStageSide. WorldParams gains `TerrainParams terrain`. World gains `WorldCell origin_` (window cell (0, 0) on the map, chunk-aligned) with origin(), to_world(Vec2i) and to_window(WorldCell) returning std::optional<Vec2i>
- core: load_window(WorldCell origin) replaces load_stage (origin floored to whole chunks): terrain from fill_terrain at the origin, no border; Stage::entry is the open cell nearest the window's centre (ring scan, rows then columns, as resume_area's); then the field, wind, spawn, fates and clock reset load_stage did. The constructor loads the window whose centre chunk holds world cell (0, 0) (origin -(chunks / 2) x kChunkSide per axis), so play starts on the open cell nearest (0, 0). The spawn stream reseeds from hash_u64(world salt ^ kHordeSeedSalt, chunk_of(origin).x, .y), so other windows draw other Dead; initial_dead stays an exact count
- core: one salt for the world: wind, slot hashes and fates use stage_seed(seed, 0) (world_salt_) for windows and layouts alike, so a layout's wind, slots and fates are bit-identical to today's and the siege baseline does not move
- core: no exit, no advance. finish_turn only counts the turn; Stage::exit, kNoExit, StageSpec's index and wall_density, stage_spec and generate_stage go (look stops reporting an exit); stage_seed stays as the salt's source. A step off the window is a wait, as a step into a wall is
- core: AreaRecord carries `WorldCell origin` instead of stage_index; resume_area rebuilds the window at record.origin as load_window does, then as today. Speculation::stage_index and World::stage_index() go (the epoch already makes a speculation from another window stale)
- core: save. WRLD writes the origin (two i64) and the terrain params in place of the stage index; SEEN writes the origin in place of its stage index and restore checks it against WRLD's; restore rebuilds a generated world with load_window(origin). kSectionVersion becomes 2 and restore refuses an older section with the issue 'written before the continuous map (PEO-118)'; no released saves exist
- test: port load_stage(i) callers to load_window at a far origin (for example (i << 20, 0)); re-pin every generated-world golden the new terrain moves (test_world's 'the pre-clock world is pinned', 'the Dead's positions are pinned', any other that fails), saying why in the commit; layout cases and siege layouts must not change. Add the cases under Tests
- app: the N key, the exit glyph and the stage number go; the HUD shows the player's world cell (x, y) where it showed the stage; the sight cache keys on origin(); the key-hint line drops N
- docs: architecture.md 'Stages' becomes 'The map (chunk.hpp, map_terrain.hpp)': a generated World is a window over the one map, no exit; the Revisits section's wording; the boundaries table's 'stage: generation is deterministic' row points at the chunk suite; wiki pages that mention the exit or N (grep)
