# PEO-046 · Random character generation: background, physique, conditions, job, where you were (D-016, D-017)

## Goal

Per D-016 and D-017. A seeded generator rolls the whole character: numeric physical stats, skills in five descriptive bands, starting items, a background with previous job (seeds skills and gear) and a where-were-you paragraph that picks a fitting start area, appearance by trait, preferences, and psychiatric, medical and physical conditions. Stub by intent; brief after PEO-008 and PEO-012.
