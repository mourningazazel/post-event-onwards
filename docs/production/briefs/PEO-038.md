# PEO-038 · Soak: a capped per-tile scent memory charged by contact (D-013)

## Goal

Per D-013. A soak value per tile, 0 to a ceiling, charged by a share of the remaining headroom while the player stands on the tile or uses its object, leaking slowly; never spreads; the Dead read it on that tile, added to human scent. Object emitters are soak. Calibration targets: floor camp about 30% at 10 days, house life 75-82% on used objects, half-life about 5 days (1 turn = 1 s). Brief just-in-time; update only touched tiles and apply leak lazily, and keep speculate/commit bit-exact.
