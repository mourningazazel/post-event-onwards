# PEO-038 · Soak: a capped per-tile scent memory charged by contact (D-013)

## Goal

Per D-013. A soak value per tile, 0 to a ceiling, charged by a share of the remaining headroom while the player stands on the tile or uses its object, leaking slowly; never spreads; the Dead read it on that tile, added to human scent. Object emitters are soak. Calibration targets: floor camp about 30% at 10 days, house life 75-82% on used objects, half-life about 5 days, per game second (D-014, D-015): charge 8.5e-7 of the headroom a second on floor and 37 times that on furniture, leak time constant 604,800 s. Charge and leak scale with seconds passed, never with steps, so running never charges faster per second. Brief just-in-time; update only touched tiles and apply leak lazily, and keep speculate/commit bit-exact.
