# PEO-149 · The window follows the player: recentre by chunks; the Dead parked and spawned per chunk (PEO-118)

## Goal

Walking never meets an edge: when the player comes within a margin of the window's edge, the window moves by whole chunks to put the player near its centre; terrain, scent, desire, occupancy and memory move with it; the Dead in chunks that leave are parked in per-chunk records with their clock, and chunks that enter bring theirs back thinned to the clock (ADR-0019) or, never visited, spawn fresh Dead at play density from the chunk's seed.

## Units

- core: shift_grid(Grid<T>&, Vec2i by, T fill) in grid.hpp: cell (x, y) takes the old cell (x + by.x, y + by.y), cells with no source take fill; rows move with memmove; allocation-free. ScentWave::shift(Vec2i by): both buffers and the direction bytes, entering cells empty, every tile marked changed (one full pull next update; shifting the flags by whole tiles is the lever if it shows) and a new token. DesireField::shift(Vec2i by) moves the log-odds; the caller rebuilds the entering strips
- core: the trigger. WorldParams::recentre_margin (cells; 0 means a quarter of the window's shorter side rounded down to whole chunks, at least one chunk). After every action of a generated world (finish_turn; never a layout), on each axis where the player is closer than the margin to an edge, the origin moves by whole chunks so the player's chunk becomes the window's centre chunk (chunks / 2, rounded down) on that axis. Deterministic from the player's cell; bumps the epoch
- core: the window's grids move: blocked and openness (shift, then fill_terrain on the entering strips), scent_, desire_ (shift, then rebuild the entering strips), occupied and reserved, the occupancy log's tiles, player_, then memory_.place(new origin)
- core: the Dead. World gains std::map<ChunkCoord, ChunkRecord> chunks_ (ChunkRecord: EventSubsteps left_at, std::vector<Dead> with pos chunk-local). A unit whose cell leaves goes to its chunk's record in horde order; every chunk that leaves gets a record, empty or not, so it never respawns. Pending landings of parked units are dropped (they stay where they stood); the rest keep their order and the landing list is remapped. Entering chunks, in (y, x) order: a record's units return thin_horde'd at since_event() and the record is erased; a chunk with none spawns: each open cell in row order holds a new Dead when hash_u64(chunk_seed(world salt, chunk), cell index, kSpawnSalt) is under spawn_chance_, with its speed from a second hash in [kMinDeadSpeed, kMaxDeadSpeed] x update_period and its fate from draw_fate at since_event() with a third. spawn_chance_ (a fraction of 2^32) is set when the first window loads: initial_dead over its open cells, so the play density holds everywhere and restore recomputes it from WRLD. Then moving, plans and the slot buffers resize and the cycle is polled again as restore_horde does. Allocation is allowed here (a rare event), never per update
- core: load_window elsewhere parks the whole old window the same way before loading, so a jump and a walk leave the same records, and a window entered again resumes its chunks. Only the constructor's first window still spawns exactly initial_dead from the stream (PEO-147); load_window spawns every chunk without a record by the hash above, as walking does. store_area, resume_area and AreaRecord retire (chunk records are the revisit store). equivalent() compares origin_, chunks_ and spawn_chance_
- test: the unit and scenario cases under Tests on 96x96 generated windows (margin one chunk); test_revisit ported from store_area / resume_area to load_window away and back, asserting the same
- test: the real-size case under Tests, in the harness PEO-145 sets up
- docs: architecture.md 'Revisits' rewritten for chunk records and 'The map' for the window and recentre; world-generation.md 'Revisits' built-so-far line; bottlenecks.md row for the recentre if it is over 1 ms on the M1 (rule 4), with a [perf] item to prepare entering chunks in the speculation

## Manual

- Run with no flags and hold a direction for about 600 steps: the view never meets an edge, the walls run on with no seam where the window moved, and Dead keep appearing ahead at the same density
- Watch for a hitch when the window moves (about every 256 steps): note the speculate ms just after and any visible pause
- Walk 600 steps back: the Dead you passed are where they stood, a few fewer; remembered walls are still remembered
- Perf observation: speculate and commit ms during the walk, and the recentre's cost (a temporary log line is fine; remove it before commit)
