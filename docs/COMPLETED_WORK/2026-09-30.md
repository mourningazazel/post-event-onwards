## PEO-058 · The Dead move in staggered slots over a 9 s cycle (D-031)

- type design · severity S2 · effort L · closed by architect · commit `640c1c6`
- goal: Per D-031. The Dead stop moving in unison. Every 9 game seconds the world polls which of the Dead can move and gives each mover one or more slots (seconds) in the cycle from a hash. At its slot a unit re-reads its best neighbour and decides; the move lands one second later, so the tile it leaves is still occupied when the next second's units decide. A calm Dead never enters a tile holding another Dead, or one reserved by a pending move, and does not pick another direction: it stays put (Ryan: calm Dead do not climb over each other; only alerted Dead push past, later). A crowd therefore files through gaps and jams a one-wide corridor. Speed becomes slots per cycle, replacing PEO-042 (held).
