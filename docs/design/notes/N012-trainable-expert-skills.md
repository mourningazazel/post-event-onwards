# N012 — Skills can be trained beyond realistic human limits

- Date: 2026-09-27
- Source: owner, in session

> Just so you know, one of the mechanics of the game is that you can train skills, albeit at a
> somewhat unrealistic rate, to do things that would be normally impossible or near impossible for
> a human, or someone who is just reading books they found to do that action. So when you're
> considering what a normal person might do, also consider what someone who is especially skilled
> at something might do.

## What this settles

- **Skills train faster than reality** and can reach **expert and beyond-human levels.**
- **Books and manuals** found in the world are a way to learn (G04 D4.2 "background-seeded, then
  use-based" gains a third source: **reading**).
- **The action catalogue must cover expert actions:** things an unskilled person can't do at all,
  and things everyone can do but experts do much better, faster, quieter or with worse tools.
