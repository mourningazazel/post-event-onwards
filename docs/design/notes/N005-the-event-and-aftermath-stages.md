# N005 — The event, the survivors, and the three aftermath stages

- Date: 2026-09-27
- Source: owner, in session

> I think that you should have it represent who was there - i think the "turning" event was all at
> once, as it is a demonic take. However, i think it is expected that about 1 in every 100 people
> did not turn, and therefore was eaten / killed, and this caused some damage and distress across
> the worldscape. Because of that, a lot of zombies will be outside as their doors and entry ways
> were left open. But i think there should be areas where the buildings were kept closed, and so
> the zombies are still in there. This will take place in 3 difficulty stages which will really
> be, 3 levels of world generation. One week after "the event", one month, and one year. The
> amount of zombies will be lower at each stage, but so will the amoutn of available supplies. You
> will meet no other NPC in this game, as it is expected you are so few in numbers that you just
> don't cross paths. But in world gen it is considered that supplies were gone through by you and
> others, as well as food spoiled etc, and things would be damaged more than before. A houses'
> windows might be broken on 1 month and year but not on 1 week. And this should be kept in mind
> during all of world gen,e specially with building and item generation, so a switch between the
> tree can be done easily.

## What this settles

- **The turning** was instant and simultaneous. It is demonic in origin.
- **Survivors.** About **1 in 100** people didn't turn. Nearly all were killed, which left damage
  and distress everywhere. The player is among the very few left.
- **Where zombies are.** A building's zombies are **the people who were there** (answers the
  follow-up on P-WO-07).
  - Many are now outside, because doors and entryways were left open.
  - Some areas stayed closed, and their zombies are still inside.
- **Difficulty = world stage:** **1 week**, **1 month** or **1 year** after the event. Each later
  stage has fewer zombies **and** fewer supplies.
- **No NPCs, ever.** Other survivors exist only as traces in world generation: looted supplies,
  and damage.
- **Accumulated time** decides spoilage, looting and damage. For example, windows are broken at 1
  month and 1 year but not at 1 week.
- **Switching between stages must be easy.** This shapes all world generation, especially
  buildings and items.
